## Austria and the European Union

The Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCAR) has been fully applicable
since 30 December 2024. According to information from the Austrian Financial
Market Authority (FMA), an offer to the public of crypto-assets other than
asset-referenced tokens or e-money tokens, or their admission to trading,
generally requires a MiCAR crypto-asset white paper unless an exemption
applies. It must be submitted to the FMA at least 20 days before its
publication.

Official guidance:

- [FMA: Issuance of crypto-assets other than ARTs or EMTs](https://www.fma.gv.at/querschnittsthemen/markets-in-crypto-assets-regulation-micar/emission-von-anderen-kryptowerten-als-art-oder-emt-und-deren-zulassung/)
- [FMA: Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation](https://www.fma.gv.at/querschnittsthemen/markets-in-crypto-assets-regulation-micar/)

The files in this project are **not a MiCAR crypto-asset white paper**. Before
any mainnet deployment, public distribution, sale, marketing as an investment,
or application for admission to trading, a specific legal and tax review in
Austria/the EU is required. A stablecoin or asset-referenced model is not
planned and would require a separate assessment.
